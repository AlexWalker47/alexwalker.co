Readout: System Stats · glass panels for your menu bar

Readout is built around one idea: your system monitor should look like part of your system. Its panels use the same Liquid Glass, rounded tiles and quiet type as Control Center, so your stats sit perfectly alongside the rest of macOS instead of looking bolted on.

  • Menu bar stats. CPU, GPU, memory, network, disk, and battery with accessory batteries, each with a live graph and a number. Readout shows only what your Mac actually has.
  • Top apps in every panel. See what is using your Mac right now.
  • A smart menu bar. When something is wrong, the menu bar briefly shows what it is.
  • Alerts when your Mac needs your attention.
  • History. Look back over the last hour, 24 hours or 7 days.
  • Trend widgets. Today, Busiest Apps, Disk Forecast, Batteries, Data Used and Slowdowns.
  • Control Center controls, plus Siri and Shortcuts.
  • A slider takes the panels from Clear to Frosted.
