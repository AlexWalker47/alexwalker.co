Readout Support

Readout is a menu bar system monitor designed to match macOS Control Center, so your CPU, GPU, memory, network, disk and battery stats look like part of your Mac. It has no Dock icon. It lives in the menu bar.

Frequently asked questions

Why does the process list show "System & other users"?
Apps from the Mac App Store run in Apple's sandbox, which limits what an app can read about other processes. Readout can list your own processes one by one. CPU used by macOS itself (for example WindowServer and kernel_task) and by other users' processes is shown as one "System & other users" row.

Why do some helper processes show as separate rows?
The same sandbox limit applies. Grouping an app's helper processes under the app (for example Safari's web content processes) needs information the sandbox does not allow, so the helpers appear as their own rows. The memory list ranks apps by resident memory, so its numbers can differ a little from Activity Monitor.

How do I open Settings?
Click any Readout item in the menu bar, then click the gear button in the panel. You can also open Readout again from the Applications folder.

How do I reset my settings?
Open Settings and choose Reset to Defaults. This puts the menu bar items, their order, refresh rate, glass strength and launch at login back to how they were at install.

How do I start Readout when I log in?
Open Settings and turn on Launch at login. It is off by default. If macOS asks for approval, allow Readout in System Settings › General › Login Items.

What does Readout need?
macOS 26 (Tahoe) or later, on an Apple silicon or Intel Mac.

Does Readout collect my data?
No. See the Privacy Policy.
