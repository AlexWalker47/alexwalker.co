Glass Stats Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 22, 2026

Glass Stats is a menu bar system monitor for macOS, made by Alex Walker and designed to match Control Center. Its privacy policy is as simple as its panels: Glass Stats does not collect anything.

What Glass Stats collects

Nothing. Glass Stats collects no personal data.

  • No analytics and no tracking.
  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising.
  • Glass Stats makes no network connections of its own.

Readings stay on your Mac

Every reading Glass Stats shows (CPU, GPU, memory and network throughput, and the process lists) is computed on your Mac and is never sent anywhere. Network throughput is measured from your Mac's own interface counters. Glass Stats does not look at the content of your network traffic.

Settings

Your settings (which meters to show, refresh rate, glass strength, launch at login) are stored locally in the app's preferences on your Mac. Settings › Reset to Defaults clears them. Deleting the app removes them.

Third parties

No data is shared with or sold to third parties. Glass Stats contains no third-party SDKs.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy: message Alex Walker on Instagram at @alex_walker47. See also Glass Stats Support.
