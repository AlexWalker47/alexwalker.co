System stats that look like they shipped with your Mac.

Glass Stats · a menu bar system monitor designed to match macOS Control Center

Glass Stats is built around one idea: your system monitor should look like part of your system. Its panels use the same Liquid Glass, rounded tiles and quiet type as Control Center, so your stats sit perfectly alongside the rest of macOS instead of looking bolted on.

  • CPU, GPU, memory and network in the menu bar, each with a live graph and a number.
  • Top apps at a glance. The CPU, GPU and memory panels show what is using your Mac right now.
  • Plain labels. "Download now" and "Upload now", not jargon.
  • Your glass, your way. A slider takes the panels from Clear to Frosted.
  • Light on resources. Watching your Mac doesn't slow it down.
