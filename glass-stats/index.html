Glass Stats

A Liquid Glass menu bar system monitor for macOS

Glass Stats puts live CPU, GPU, memory and network meters in your Mac's menu bar. Each meter shows a small live graph and a number. Click one to open a Liquid Glass panel with history charts, per-core load, top processes and apps, GPU memory and network totals.
